Speech models in this folder

whisper-tiny and whisper-base are the open multilingual Whisper speech-recognition models, published under the
MIT License (Copyright (c) 2022 OpenAI), in the ONNX conversions published by the ONNX Community for use with
Transformers.js. They are used unmodified, on the player's device only, to turn spoken answers into text.

The runtime in js/vendor (Transformers.js and ONNX Runtime Web) is published under the Apache License 2.0 and the
MIT License respectively; see js/vendor/LICENSE-transformers.txt.
